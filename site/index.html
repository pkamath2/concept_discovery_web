Discovering Sound-Source Concepts in Text-to-Audio Diffusion Models Using Sparse Autoencoders

Supplementary audio examples — single-concept steering

We steer individual, interpretable SAE features in the residual stream of Stable Audio Open. For each prompt we sweep the steering strength alpha (columns) across three DiT layers (rows), at a single fixed denoising timestep t (shown beside the prompt). alpha = 0 is the unsteered generation.

Best viewed on Chrome or Firefox.

  5. Water dripping.
